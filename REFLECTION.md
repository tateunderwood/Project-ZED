# Reflection — TrueContract (Project Zed)

## The technique I learned: grouping a dropdown with `<optgroup>`

The AI helped me understand how to make different sections of the dropbox by making more
addGroup and ordering it how i want them to appear in the dropbox. The MVP is added first so
it appears at the top, .sort allows for it to be sorted with the most at the top and the least
at the bottom, such as mvpCount(b) - mvpCount(a). rest keeps the players from repeating in
different sections.

I needed this so that way i could be able to sort through the top players in the league quickly. If this is something that i share to people outside of myself to use then they will use it for quarterbacks that influence them the most and are talked about the most, typically being the ones with the most MVPs

If i delete the mvpGroup then the dropdown goes back to the plain Active vs Retired list, so the site doesn't shut down and stays functioning. 

## Judgment calls the AI couldn't make for me


The Super Bowls for only starting quarterbacks was because i feel that super bowls are most deserved by the players who actually made it happen and actually played when it mattered. 

I stopped at the Year 2000 because i didn't want to get too carried away with the assignment and keep it condensed for the sake of keeping the assignment not too overwhelming but relevant to most football fans who want to see how their favorite quarterback stacks up against others. 

It's a weird blip to just not have any cap for one year, so i just went with the previous 123 million dollar cap from 2009. This just made the code easier to keep consistent and made the most sense. 

I feel like seeing totals can just blow things out of proportion. Seeing someone who makes way more money than another person makes you think that they are way better than someone else, until you realize that one of them has been in the league an extra 10 years as a journeyman backup while the other is an up and coming super star that is just early in his career. Seeing averages makes an easier picture to what impact a quarterback would have on any given Sunday 

All the choices serve the reader as they add key details and adjustments that make for a more transparent TrueValue, and paint a better picture of what we are actually measuring.
